# Hue Landscape — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Hue Landscape Pte. Ltd. provides landscaping, garden maintenance and outdoor improvement services in Singapore. It serves homeowners, condominium and MCST management, commercial property managers and institutional customers.

The proposed homepage gives its comprehensive service offering a clearer structure, supports capabilities with relevant project evidence and creates more direct enquiry routes for different customer groups.

## Main Improvements

1. **Stronger opening and service hierarchy**  
   Introduce a more prominent visual statement and prioritise key service areas, including maintenance, tree care, landscape construction and specialist green features. This makes the broad offering easier to understand without allowing individual services to compete equally for attention.

2. **Project stories with visual evidence**  
   Support portfolio photographs with verified project types, work scopes and outcomes where available. This gives prospective customers more useful context than client-related imagery alone and helps them assess experience relevant to their property.

3. **Audience-specific enquiry routes**  
   Give homeowners, MCSTs and commercial property managers distinct starting points with relevant service information and clear enquiry actions. Include guidance for sending site photographs through WhatsApp to help customers explain their requirements.

## Design Improvements

- Establish a clearer sequence from the opening proposition to services, project evidence and enquiries.
- Use consistent headings and service layouts to make the extensive offering easier to scan.
- Give project photography and relevant contact actions greater visual emphasis.

## Implementation Status

This summary documents the proposed redesign direction. Implementation has not been verified against a redesigned homepage draft, and project stories require confirmed information.
